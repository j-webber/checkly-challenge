What just happened

Synthetic catches what passive misses

Passive alerting
  • Waits for traffic patterns to trigger thresholds
  • Works when failure volume is high enough
  • Silent on low-traffic paths during off-hours
  • Your customer is the canary
Synthetic monitoring
  • Actively checks for truth on a fixed schedule
  • Detects failure at zero user traffic
  • Isolates backend from frontend from network
  • You are the canary

Grafana tells you how your system is behaving.
Checkly tells you whether it still works.

How AI was used

Fast scaffold. Verified reality. Owned the reasoning.

01
Scaffolded check structure quickly

Used Claude to generate the initial ApiCheck and BrowserCheck constructs and wire the checkly.config.ts. Cut 30 min of boilerplate to under 5.

02
Verified every route, selector, and payload against the live app

Probed GET /api/products, tested POST /api/cart with real payloads, scraped the JS bundle for data-cy values and form field ids. No generated guesses trusted.

03
Owned the check-selection reasoning personally

AI doesn't know what's on the revenue path for this prospect. The decision to skip 7 services and write 3 specific checks — that's a judgment call, not a prompt.
